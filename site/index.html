Searching Well with AI: A Friendly Manual

Part of The Massive AI Manual series by Dylan Asdale — published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC

Who this is for

You. Whatever your job is — nurse, teacher, plumber, small-business owner, student, parent — you now have two superpowers sitting next to each other: search engines and AI chatbots. Most people use one and ignore the other, or use both badly. This short manual teaches you how to use them together so you get answers that are actually true, not just answers that sound confident.

No jargon. No condescension. If something has a technical name, we’ll give it to you once, then use plain English from there on.

What you’ll learn

  1. When search beats the chatbot — and when the chatbot beats search. (Chapter 1)
  2. Crafting searches that find the good stuff — operators, phrasing, and habits. (Chapter 2)
  3. Asking AI for sources, then verifying them — because AI invents citations surprisingly often. (Chapter 3)
  4. Reverse image search — finding where a picture really came from. (Chapter 4)
  5. Date checks on stale advice — a 30-second habit that saves you from outdated answers. (Chapter 5)
  6. The five-question reliability rubric — one page you can screenshot and keep. (Chapter 6)

How to use this manual

  • Read chapters 1 and 2 in one sitting (~15 minutes). They change how you search immediately.
  • Read chapters 3 and 4 the next day. They’re the “trust but verify” core.
  • Chapter 5 is a habit, not a lesson — practice it every time you look something up.
  • Keep chapter 6 handy. It’s a checklist, not homework.

Companion guides
